“Leave. Before I change my mind. In fact, you show up again I’m likely to finish the job.”

“Fine. If you need me again …” he stood up and moved toward the door.

“I mean it. Come back here and I will have you killed.”

He laughed. “Surely you know that’s not possible.”

“Besides you already have enough blood on your hands,” he said as the door closed.
